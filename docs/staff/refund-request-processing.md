> Sample procedure written for an academic project. It is not a Kenya Airways internal document.

## When to use

A customer asks for a refund of a whole ticket or of unused sectors.

## Before you start (what to check)

- The caller is verified (see Call Handling and Caller Verification).
- Where the ticket was bought. Tickets bought through a travel agency are refunded by that agency.
- The fare rules on the ticket: refundable, refundable with a fee, or non-refundable (taxes may still be refundable).
- Whether the flight was cancelled or significantly changed by KQ. Then the customer's options are wider than the fare rules.
- For tickets bought in the United States, the Customer Service Plan rules (24-hour free cancellation and refund timelines).

## Steps

1. Confirm which sectors the customer wants refunded.
2. Check the fare rules and work out what is refundable. Don't promise an amount until the fare rules are checked.
3. If KQ cancelled or significantly changed the flight, offer the options in the Customer Service Plan and Conditions of Carriage (rebooking, reroute, travel credit or refund) and let the customer choose.
4. Explain that submitting the refund request cancels the unused itinerary. Get the customer's confirmation.
5. Submit the request through the official refund form, or guide the customer to it.
6. Give the customer the refund request reference.
7. Record the request and the customer's choice in the booking remarks.

## What to tell the customer

- The expected processing time, as stated on the refund page. Card refunds can be faster.
- That travel agency bookings are refunded through the agency.
- That the refund follows the fare rules, and which parts are refundable.

## Escalate if

- The refund is overdue beyond the published processing time.
- The customer disputes the fare rules or a KQ disruption is involved and the fare rules seem to block a refund.
- A chargeback or legal action is mentioned.

## Based on (public policy and section)

- Refund Request Form and Policy: how to apply and processing time.
- Customer Service Plan: refunds and cancellations for US bookings.
- Conditions of Carriage: refunds article.
