> Sample procedure written for an academic project. It is not a Kenya Airways internal document.

## When to use

A member asks about missing miles, their tier, or tier benefits.

## Before you start (what to check)

- The member is verified, with their Asante Rewards number.
- For missing miles: the flight, date and ticket number, and whether the member number was on the booking.

## Steps

1. **Missing miles:** check that the flight has been flown and that the booking class earns miles under the programme's earning rules. If eligible, submit a missing-miles request with the ticket number and boarding details, and give the member the request reference.
2. **Tier queries:** read the member's current tier and progress from the member record. Explain tier benefits using the programme pages, such as priority check-in and lounge access for higher tiers.
3. **Benefits not applied:** if a benefit was missed at the airport, record the flight and what happened, and pass it to the loyalty team.
4. Record the request in the member's notes.

## What to tell the customer

- The request reference and when to check again.
- That miles are credited only for eligible fares and flown sectors.

## Escalate if

- A request has been open longer than the programme's stated processing time.
- The member reports suspicious activity on their account. Treat it as a possible account takeover.

## Based on (public policy and section)

- Asante Rewards programme pages: earning rules and tier benefits.
- Check-in Policy: priority services for Asante Gold and Platinum members.
