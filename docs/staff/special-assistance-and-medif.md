> Sample procedure written for an academic project. It is not a Kenya Airways internal document.

## When to use

A customer needs medical clearance, oxygen, a wheelchair, a service dog, an unaccompanied minor service, or the KQ Ezesha discount.

## Before you start (what to check)

- The travel date. Medical clearance, oxygen and stretcher requests have minimum notice periods on the medical page.
- Whether the flights are operated by KQ. Some services apply on KQ-operated flights only.
- Any existing special service requests (SSRs) on the booking.

## Steps

1. Ask what the customer needs and for which flights.
2. **Medical clearance:** if the condition is on the medical page's MEDIF list, send the customer the MEDIF form. It must be completed in English, signed by their doctor, and sent to KQ's medical team before the deadline on the page. A new MEDIF is needed for the return.
3. **Oxygen, stretcher or powered medical device:** check the extra notice, escort and battery rules on the medical page, and that Engineering approval is requested in time. Tell the customer about any fees listed there.
4. **Wheelchair:** add the right SSR to each sector. Tell the customer one mobility device travels free.
5. **Service dog:** confirm it is a trained service dog (emotional support animals are not accepted), add the SSR, and check the documents on the pet carriage page.
6. **Unaccompanied minor:** check the child's age against the minors page, confirm all flights are KQ-operated with no overnight connection, and send the UM form. Tell the customer the fee rules on the page.
7. **KQ Ezesha:** confirm the customer holds an Ezesha card, and transfer the call to the Ezesha office, which handles these bookings.
8. Record every request and its status in the booking remarks.

## What to tell the customer

- What they must send, by when, and that the service is confirmed only after clearance.
- Any fees, quoted from the public page.

## Escalate if

- The deadline has passed but travel is urgent.
- The medical team declines clearance and the customer disputes it.
- Equipment needs approval you can't confirm.

## Based on (public policy and section)

- Medical and Special Needs: MEDIF, PMC, oxygen, stretchers, devices.
- Unaccompanied Minors: ages, conditions, form, fee.
- KQ Ezesha Programme: eligibility and booking route.
- Pet Carriage: service dog rules.
