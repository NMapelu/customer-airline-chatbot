> Sample procedure written for an academic project. It is not a Kenya Airways internal document.

## When to use

A customer's checked bag did not arrive, arrived damaged, or has items missing.

## Before you start (what to check)

- Whether the customer reported it at the arrival airport and has a file reference.
- The flight number, date, and bag tag numbers.
- The date the customer arrived. Written claims have a deadline, set on the baggage page.

## Steps

1. If there is no file reference, tell the customer a report must be filed. Guide them to the arrival desk or the baggage tracing route on the KQ page.
2. With a file reference, check the bag's status in WorldTracer and tell the customer what you find.
3. For a delayed bag, check First Needs Allowance eligibility using the rules on the baggage page: away from home, bag not expected within 24 hours, receipts for essentials only. Note the limits by cabin and the child rules on that page.
4. Explain that First Needs Allowance is paid against a signed release, and is deducted from final compensation if the bag is lost.
5. For a damaged bag or missing items, explain the written claim deadline and what to include (file reference, tags, receipts, photos).
6. Record the case, what was explained, and any First Needs Allowance request in the remarks.

## What to tell the customer

- Their file reference and how to track the bag.
- The written claim deadline.
- That KQ is not liable for valuables, money, jewellery, fragile or perishable items in checked bags.

## Escalate if

- The bag is untraced after the period on the baggage page, or the customer asks for final compensation.
- The customer disputes a First Needs Allowance decision.
- A mobility device or medical equipment is delayed or damaged. Treat it as urgent.

## Based on (public policy and section)

- Delayed, Lost or Damaged Baggage: reporting, tracing, First Needs Allowance, claims.
- Conditions of Carriage (Baggage): liability limits and exclusions.
