> Sample procedure written for an academic project. It is not a Kenya Airways internal document.

## When to use

A customer calls after being denied boarding because a flight was overbooked, or asks what happens if a flight is overbooked.

## Before you start (what to check)

- Whether the customer volunteered to give up their seat or was denied boarding involuntarily.
- The reason recorded on the booking. Denied boarding for late check-in, missing travel documents or safety reasons is not overbooking.
- The route. Some countries set their own compensation rules.

## Steps

1. Confirm what happened and when, and the reason recorded at the airport.
2. If the customer was denied boarding for overbooking, confirm they have been rebooked. If not, follow Missed Connection and Involuntary Rebooking.
3. Check the denied boarding article of the Conditions of Carriage and any rules for the route.
4. Explain the options to the customer. Don't promise a compensation amount on the phone.
5. Open a case with the customer's details, flight, and what the airport offered.
6. Record everything in the booking remarks.

## What to tell the customer

- That their case has been opened, and its reference.
- What the Conditions of Carriage say about denied boarding.
- If the reason was late check-in or missing documents, which rule applied.

## Escalate if

- The customer was denied boarding involuntarily and asks for compensation.
- The customer has onward connections or special needs.

## Based on (public policy and section)

- Conditions of Carriage: denied boarding and schedules articles.
- Customer Service Plan: overbooking (US flights).
