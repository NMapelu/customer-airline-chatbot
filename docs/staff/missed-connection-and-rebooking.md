> Sample procedure written for an academic project. It is not a Kenya Airways internal document.

## When to use

A flight is delayed or cancelled, or a delay caused the customer to miss a KQ connection.

## Before you start (what to check)

- The disruption on the booking: delay, cancellation, or misconnection.
- Whether the customer has already used self-rebooking. It can be used once per disruption.
- Whether the system has already rebooked the customer automatically.
- The customer's cabin class and baggage allowance. These stay the same after rebooking.

## Steps

1. Confirm the disrupted flight and the customer's final destination.
2. Tell the customer about self-rebooking if they haven't used it. Help them if they prefer you to do it.
3. Offer the next suitable KQ flights in the same class. Use the rebooking window in the Self Rebooking FAQs.
4. If no suitable KQ flight exists, check reroute options under the Conditions of Carriage.
5. Rebook and reissue the ticket. Confirm checked bags are transferred to the new flight.
6. If the customer would rather not travel, follow Refund Request Processing.
7. For long delays, check whether meals or a hotel apply under the Conditions of Carriage and the Customer Service Plan, and record what was provided.
8. Record the disruption, the option chosen, and anything provided in the booking remarks.

## What to tell the customer

- The new flight details and that their bags will follow.
- That the class and baggage allowance stay the same.
- If they change the unaffected return leg, that it is handled through their agent or the contact centre.

## Escalate if

- No option reaches the destination within a reasonable time.
- The customer has a medical need, an unaccompanied minor is travelling, or the customer would be stranded overnight.
- The customer claims compensation under a regulation (for example, US DOT or EU rules).

## Based on (public policy and section)

- Self Rebooking FAQs: eligibility, window, once-per-disruption rule, bags.
- Customer Service Plan: delays, cancellations and significant changes for US flights.
- Conditions of Carriage: schedules, cancellations and rerouting.
