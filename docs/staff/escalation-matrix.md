> Sample procedure written for an academic project. It is not a Kenya Airways internal document. Desk names are examples.

## When to use

A case is outside what you can resolve on the call, or another procedure says "escalate".

## Before you start (what to check)

- The case is recorded in the booking remarks or a case number, with what the customer asked for.

## Steps

1. Find the case type in the table and send it to that desk.
2. Tell the customer who will handle it and when to expect contact.
3. Record the escalation, the desk, and the time.

| Case | Send to | Urgency |
|------|---------|---------|
| Refund overdue or disputed | Refunds desk | Normal |
| Missed connection with no reasonable option, or customer stranded | Duty supervisor | Urgent |
| Involuntary denied boarding compensation | Customer relations | Normal |
| Lost bag, or final baggage compensation | Baggage services | Normal |
| Delayed or damaged mobility or medical equipment | Baggage services and Special assistance | Urgent |
| Medical clearance disputes or late requests | Special assistance (medical team) | Urgent |
| Asante Rewards account takeover suspected | Loyalty team and IT security | Urgent |
| Goodwill approval | Duty supervisor | Normal |
| Legal, media, regulator, safety or discrimination | Customer relations manager | Urgent |
| Personal data request | Data protection contact (Privacy Policy) | Normal |

## What to tell the customer

- Which team will handle it and the case reference.
- For anything outside the call centre, the official KQ contact details from the Contact Us page.

## Escalate if

- An urgent case gets no response from the desk. Go to the duty supervisor.

## Based on (public policy and section)

- Customer Service Plan: complaint and disruption handling.
- Contact Us: official contact channels.
