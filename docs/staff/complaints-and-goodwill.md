> Sample procedure written for an academic project. It is not a Kenya Airways internal document. Goodwill amounts are set by supervisors, so none are given here.

## When to use

A customer makes a complaint about service, a disruption, or staff, or asks for a goodwill gesture.

## Before you start (what to check)

- The booking and anything already recorded about the issue.
- Whether the customer is entitled to something under policy (refund, rebooking, First Needs Allowance). Entitlements come first. Goodwill is extra.

## Steps

1. Listen to the whole complaint without interrupting, then summarise it back to the customer.
2. Apologise for the experience. Don't admit liability.
3. Check whether a policy entitlement applies and, if so, handle it with the matching procedure.
4. If no entitlement applies but the experience fell short, you may propose a goodwill gesture (for example, Asante Rewards miles). Get supervisor approval before offering it.
5. Open a complaint case with the facts, what the customer asked for, and what was offered.
6. Give the customer the case reference and when to expect a reply.

## What to tell the customer

- What happens next and when.
- Never promise compensation that hasn't been approved.

## Escalate if

- The customer mentions legal action, the media, or a regulator.
- The complaint involves safety, discrimination, or staff misconduct.
- The customer rejects the outcome.

## Based on (public policy and section)

- Customer Service Plan: complaint handling and response times (US flights).
- Conditions of Carriage: liability.
