> Sample procedure written for an academic project. It is not a Kenya Airways internal document. Fees are not listed because they depend on the fare bought.

## When to use

A customer asks whether they can change or cancel a ticket, or what happens if they miss a flight.

## Before you start (what to check)

- The fare rules attached to the ticket. They always take priority over this guide.
- Whether KQ caused the change (delay, cancellation, schedule change). If so, use Missed Connection and Involuntary Rebooking instead.

## Steps

1. Read the ticket's fare rules for changes, cancellation, and no-show.
2. **Change:** tell the customer whether changes are allowed, the change fee in the fare rules, and that any fare difference applies.
3. **Cancellation:** tell the customer whether the ticket is refundable, partly refundable, or non-refundable, and that some taxes may still be refundable. Then follow Refund Request Processing.
4. **No-show:** explain the no-show rule in the fare rules. Missing a sector can cancel the rest of the itinerary, so the customer should call before departure if their plans change.
5. **US bookings made directly with KQ:** check whether the 24-hour free cancellation in the Customer Service Plan applies.
6. Record what you explained in the booking remarks.

## What to tell the customer

- The exact rule from their fare, not a general answer.
- That KQ-caused disruptions follow the disruption rules, not the fare rules.

## Escalate if

- The fare rules are unclear or conflict with what the customer was told at booking.

## Based on (public policy and section)

- Conditions of Carriage: fares, tickets, refunds and the sequence in which coupons are used.
- Refund Request Form and Policy.
- Customer Service Plan: 24-hour cancellation (US).
